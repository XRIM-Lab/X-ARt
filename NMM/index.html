About this Series

An ongoing exploration of Nature's invisible forces through performance and augmented reality.

The Nature Series is a sequence of performances and installations that use augmented reality to make visible the "fields of force" and "flows of materials" of Nature — the invisible energies that affect every aspect of our lives.

Each performance is site-specific, combining ephemeral land art with digital augmentations in AR.
