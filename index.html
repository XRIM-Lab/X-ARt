About X-ARt

Using augmented reality technologies, we metaphorically reveal the ‘fields of force’ and ‘flows of materials’ of Nature, to arouse a greater appreciation of Nature and its invisible forces that affect every aspect of our lives.

Our work is a blend of site-specific installations, performance art, and technological experimentation, always aiming to bridge the gap between the digital and the natural world.
