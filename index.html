About artrio

A multi-disciplinary art-tech collective.

artrio is an arts-tech collective that explores the relationships between humans, nature, and living traditions.

artrio brings together artists, designers, engineers and scientists, to create site-specific live performances and installations at the intersection of art and technology, opening up new forms of artistic expression.

Until December 2025, artrio operated under the name 「X-ARt」.

Our approach

Augmented reality-driven creative research.

Using real-time AR systems developed by XRIM Lab, we create performances, installations, and exhibitions that combine spatial computing, visual arts, and traditional embodied practices. Using real-time AR systems developed by XRIM Lab for live performances, artrio visualises the usually imperceptible dynamics that link all things of nature.

Our works explore how people relate to nature, place, tradition, and practices. Each project comprises documentation, and, at times, active participation from the local communities, creating artworks that are site-specific not only physically and digitally, but also philosophically.

Many of our works take place in natural settings or cultural sites. Minimising our environmental impact is thus an essential component in our creative process. As such, we practice creative circularity, ensuring that materials are repatriated, reused, or recycled wherever possible
